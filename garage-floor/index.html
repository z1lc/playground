Three ways to treat a garage floor

    What the map says

    Best value

    The three penetrating sealers sit far left: no film, nothing to breathe or touch but concrete, and under $750 over ten years. Densifier + repellent buys the most protection, and adds water repellency the densifier alone lacks.

    Best protection

    Polyaspartic flake tops the chart, with good wet grip from the flake. It costs $3,000–5,500, and the slab needs a moisture test first.

    Looks cheap, isn’t

    Acrylic and DIY epoxy kits need redoing every few years, so ten years of either costs more than a penetrating sealer, for less protection.

    Mid-priced, worst for kids

    PVC tiles are the one floor with documented plasticizer migration into household dust.

    Gym mats

    What your kids will breathe and touch

    Mats go on top of whichever floor you pick, and they are the part of the gym kids are closest to. They breathe what a mat gives off, touch it, and get it on their hands and in their mouths. Products here are graded on proof: what the maker or seller can show about what is in the mat, for each of those routes, not how it is marketed.
